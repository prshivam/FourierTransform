Method

From pixels to a closed-form drawing

  • 01

    Find the edges

    The photo is blurred, then Sobel gradients with non-maximum suppression leave a one-pixel outline.

  • 02

    Split into sections

    Edge pixels are chained into strokes and clustered into regions — eyes, brows, nose, mouth, hair, jaw.

  • 03

    Transform

    Each section is resampled and run through an FFT, giving the coefficients ak, bk, ck, dk.

  • 04

    Rebuild

    Keep the first N terms, sum the rotating circles, and the pen redraws the face. The error is the energy you dropped.
